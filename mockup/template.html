What if 2nd Street belonged to people?

Blockwise simulates a weekday in San Francisco's Financial District and upper SoMa, using real buildings and streets from OpenStreetMap. Every dot is a sim with a commute, a lunch spot and coworkers. Change a street or a block, and the city replans around your edit. Every result is measured against the unchanged city.

This is a design mockup. The sims run on simple rules and the numbers are illustrative.
