Three ways the website could look

Same features in all three: home page, workspaces, invite links with roles, plans with a workflow stage, live presence and activity, and one click into the shared 3D simulation. Only the design differs.

Studio

1 · Studio

A light, calm SaaS workspace in the style of Linear or Notion. A sidebar lists your workspaces; plans are cards with map thumbnails.

Light · sidebar · card grid
Skyline

2 · Skyline

Dark and cinematic, matching the 3D simulator. A big city-map hero, a “mission control” plan list with live presence, and full-bleed plan pages.

Dark · glass · matches the sim
Civic Board

3 · Civic Board

An editorial look: warm paper and serif headlines. Plans sit on a drag-and-drop board (Draft → In review → Approved) that organizes the team’s workflow.

Editorial · kanban workflow

All three share one account and the same live data, so a workspace made in one shows up in the others. To try collaboration on one computer, use Add another person in the account menu: it opens a second tab as someone else.
