The Human Project

What if 2nd Street belonged to people?

Modeling urban society with agent swarms.

A live simulation of a weekday in San Francisco's Financial District and SoMa, built from real OpenStreetMap buildings and streets. Every dot is a person with a commute, a lunch spot and coworkers. Change a street or a block and the whole swarm replans. Every result is measured against the unchanged city.

Design mockup. The agents follow simple rules and the numbers are illustrative.
