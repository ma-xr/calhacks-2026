The Human Project · Modeling urban society with agent swarms

What if 2nd Street belonged to people?

A swarm of 6,000 agents lives a weekday in San Francisco's Financial District and upper SoMa, built from real OpenStreetMap buildings and streets. Each agent has a commute, a lunch spot and coworkers. Change a street or a block and the swarm replans around your edit. Every result is measured against the unchanged city.

Design mockup. Agents follow simple rules and the numbers are illustrative.
