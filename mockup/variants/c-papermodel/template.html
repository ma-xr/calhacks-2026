      CommonGround · modeling urban society with agent swarms

      What if 2nd Street belonged to people?

      This is a working model of a weekday in San Francisco's Financial District and SoMa, built from real OpenStreetMap buildings and streets. About 6,000 agents each have a commute, a lunch spot and coworkers. Change a street or a block and every agent replans its day. Each result is measured against the unchanged city.

      Design mockup. Agents follow simple rules and the numbers are illustrative.
