# Barcelona Superblocks (Superilles): Poblenou 2016, Sant Antoni 2018–19, Eixample green axes 2022–23

## Context
Barcelona's Eixample grid (113 m blocks) has very high traffic density, about 5,500 veh/km² in the most congested areas ([Contesti, FUP journal](https://oajournals.fupress.net/index.php/contesti/article/view/14178)). The Eixample central grid carries ~350,000 vehicles/day, with Aragó alone at 85,000/day ([Ara, 21 Apr 2022, citing city mobility study](https://en.ara.cat/society/barcelona-traffic-car-pedestrian-superilla-plan_1_4345902.amp.html)). The Superblock model (BCNecologia, Salvador Rueda) groups 3x3 blocks, pushes through-traffic to the perimeter, and limits interior streets to 10 km/h for local access ([ASPB Salut als Carrers 2021](https://www.aspb.cat/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/ASPB_salut-carrers-avaluacio-superilles.pdf)).

## Health impact assessment (modelled, not measured): Mueller et al. 2020
[Mueller N. et al., "Changing the urban design of cities for health: The superblock model," *Environment International* 134 (2020)](https://ddd.uab.cat/record/284134); figures from the [ISGlobal press release](https://www.isglobal.org/documents/10179/7035461/NdP+Superilles+CAT/f42a52b0-8e4d-426f-a136-10d60083e592):
| Output (full 503-superblock scenario) | Value |
|---|---|
| Premature deaths prevented / yr | **667** (95% CI 235–1,098) |
| By pathway | NO2 291; traffic noise 163; heat 117; green space 60 (Eixample only); physical activity 36 |
| Life expectancy | +~200 days per resident (average) |
| Economic value | €1.7 bn / yr |
| NO2 (annual mean) | 47 → 36 µg/m³ (−24%) |
| Traffic noise | 54 → 51 dB (−5.4%) |
| Mode shift | ~230,000 of 1.19 M weekday private-motor trips shift to transit/active modes (~19%) |

## Caveats / confounds
- Sant Antoni's change coincided with the reopening of the renovated Sant Antoni market (2018). Footfall +16% cannot be separated from that.
- NO2 measurements are short campaigns (weeks) at one or two sites, corrected to one reference station. Displacement onto perimeter streets was flagged but not quantified. Horta showed no effect.
- Eixample −17% / −25% figures are city self-reports. Rent studies (12–15%) are press-reported DiD on listing prices, not transaction or tenant data.
- Mueller 2020 is a scenario HIA assuming full build-out of 503 superblocks plus city traffic reduction. It is not observed data, and the authors themselves urge caution.

## What our model should reproduce
| Parameter / output | Target |
|---|---|
| Treated-street traffic | −80% or more on the street itself (Comte Borrell −82/83%) |
| `traffic_evaporation_median` | Area-level traffic −17% to −21% over 2–4 years at Sant Antoni, i.e. most of the treated-street volume does *not* reappear inside the zone. City design assumption was >20% evaporation at full scale ([Contesti](https://oajournals.fupress.net/index.php/contesti/article/view/14178)). Perimeter streets should show *some* increase (Mallorca St forecast to saturate). |
| Air quality | Treated junction NO2 −25%, PM10 −17%. Channelling streets may *increase*. |
| `ped_footfall_uplift` | +16% visitors in the year of opening (confounded by the market). Use +10–16%. |
| `ped_street_retail_uplift` | Shop count flat in a mature retail area (Sant Antoni); +31% ground-floor activities in an emerging area (Poblenou). Spending recovered faster than control areas. Central value: modest (+0–10% sales) in mature cores. |
| `walker_vs_driver_visit_ratio` | Only 5–10% of shoppers arrive by car, while merchants believe 10–20% do (AMB 2017, cited in the [city report](https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/superilles/sites/default/files/20220322_comerc_serveis_web.pdf)); 6.8% by car at Sant Antoni. |
| `rent_walkability_elasticity` | Residential rents +12–15% within 50–100 m, decaying to ~0–4% by 500 m. Commercial prime rents can roughly double (+96%) within 3–5 years. Use a distance-decay kernel with ~100–200 m half-distance. |
| Health / City Value | Full-scale HIA: 667 deaths/yr avoided, €1.7 bn/yr ÷ Barcelona pop. ~1.6 M (unverified here) ≈ €1,050 per resident per year (derived) at full city build-out. Scale down linearly by treated area and exposed population. |
