# Case Study: Car-Free Market Street (Better Market Street Quick Build), San Francisco, 2020

**Relevance to our sim:** The closest real-world analogue to the core intervention. It sits directly inside our study area (Market St is the north edge of upper SoMa / south edge of FiDi). Use it as the primary calibration target for `muni_speed_change_market_car_free`, `parallel_street_max_slowdown_market_car_free` and `cycling_change_market_car_free`.

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Start date | Wed **Jan 29, 2020** | [SFMTA FAQ](https://www.sfmta.com/en/node/21169) |
| Eastbound car-free extent | **10th St → Main St** | same |
| Westbound car-free extent | **Steuart St → Van Ness Ave** | same |

| Curb / loading | ~100 new cross-street loading zones; peak-hour loading bans (no south-side EB loading 6:00–9:30 a.m.; no north-side WB loading 3:00–7:30 p.m.) | [SFMTA FAQ](https://www.sfmta.com/en/node/21169) |
| Other | Painted safety zones at 8 intersections; bike intersection fixes at 8th, Page, Battery, Valencia | [SFCTA blog](https://www.sfcta.org/blogs/market-street-goes-car-free) |
| Cost | ~$3.5M quick build (local general fund) | [SFMTA FAQ](https://www.sfmta.com/en/node/21169) |
| Enforcement | CVC 22101(d), $238 fine + 1 DMV point | same |
| SFMTA's own diversion forecast | ~**100 cars/hour** expected to shift to Mission St | same |

## 5. Caveats / confounds

1. **COVID.** SF shelter-in-place began **Mar 16–17, 2020**, about **7 weeks** after launch. All clean "after" data comes from Feb 1 to mid-Mar 2020. SFMTA itself caveats its bike figure as "until Shelter in Place ordered" ([SFMTA Board 2021](https://sfmta.com/media/25169/download)). Any 2021+ comparison (the −14% travel time and −40% collisions) is confounded by citywide traffic and ridership collapse. As of mid-2024, Muni ridership was still about 35% below 2019 ([RPD/SFMTA GGP report citing the Controller, Sep 2024](https://sfrecpark.org/DocumentCenter/View/28988/Golden-Gate-Park-Annual-Access-and-Safety-Report-FY24)).
2. **Short window.** INRIX used about 3 weeks after vs 2 weeks before, Tue–Thu only. February is seasonal, and there was a novelty/avoidance effect.
3. **Bundled treatments.** The Muni-only lane extension, loading bans and turn bans launched together. The 12% cannot be attributed to car removal alone.
4. **Low pre-existing car volume.** Market already had heavy turn restrictions (2015 Safer Market St). Only ~200–400 private cars/hr used it in the peak direction, so the diverted volume was small. This is why parallel-street effects were small. **Do not extrapolate the ~1% effect to a street carrying through-traffic.**
5. "Up to 12%" is a best-line figure. The average was about 6%.

## 7. What our model should reproduce (validation targets)

Scenario: close Market St (Embarcadero→5th within our bbox) to private autos and TNCs, but keep buses, taxis and commercial vehicles.

| Param key | Target | Acceptable band | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| `muni_speed_change_market_car_free` | **+6%** average Muni speed on Market (≈ −6% travel time) | +4% to +13.6% (12% travel-time cut = 1/0.88−1 ≈ +13.6% speed, best line only). Long-run press figure −14% travel time is confounded | [SFMTA 2021](https://sfmta.com/media/25169/download), [Chronicle 2020](https://www.sfchronicle.com/bayarea/article/Results-of-car-free-Market-Street-so-so-for-SF-15087210.php) |

Some parallels (Howard, Folsom) may **speed up** by 1–10% | [INRIX](https://inrix.com/blog/full-car-ban-on-market-street-little-impact-on-surrounding-roads/) |
| `cycling_change_market_car_free` | **+25%** bikes on Market (peak >800/hr from 650/hr) | +20% (day 1) to +25% (first 6 weeks) | [SFMTA 2021](https://sfmta.com/media/25169/download); [SF Examiner](https://sfexaminer.com/news/bike-ridership-up-20-percent-on-first-day-of-car-free-market-street) |

**Sim realism notes:** if the sim produces >5% average slowdown on Mission/Howard/Folsom for a Market closure, it is over-penalising. The real diverted volume was small because Market already carried few cars. If a user also pedestrianises a parallel street (e.g., Mission), the model should not reuse these numbers. That is a new capacity loss with a much larger diverted volume.
