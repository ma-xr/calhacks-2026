# Pontevedra, Spain: car-free centre (1999→)

## Context
Pontevedra is a compact Galician provincial capital of ~74,000 (1999) to ~83,000 (2019) residents (INE padrón via [Foro-Ciudad](https://www.foro-ciudad.com/pontevedra/pontevedra/habitantes.html)). By the late 1990s ~52,000 vehicles circulated in the city daily and 14,000 cars/day used a single central street ([Citiscope / Eco-Business, 2017](https://www.eco-business.com/news/how-a-city-in-spain-got-rid-of-its-cars/)). Before the change, public space was under 1/6 of street area ([Ecosostenible / Wolters Kluwer, 2015](https://qinnova.uned.es/archivos_publicos/qweb_paginas/16993/bp2015pontevedramodelociudadecosostenible.pdf)). Mayor Miguel Anxo Fernández Lores (elected 1999) pedestrianized the historic centre by decree, then extended traffic calming citywide.

## Caveats / confounds
- Nearly every figure is city-reported. Baselines (1996, 1997, 1999) and geographies (historic centre, centre, city) vary, which produces −70% vs −92% vs −97% for "centre traffic." There is no peer-reviewed before/after evaluation.
- Population growth (+12% municipal, 1999–2019) is real per INE but may reflect regional dynamics. Attributing it to the car-free policy is a narrative claim, not an estimate.
- Pontevedra is small and compact (most trips under 2 km). The share of trips that can shift to walking is much larger than for regional commuters into SF FiDi.
- Zero-death claims cover different windows (since 2009 / 2011 / "13 years").

## What our model should reproduce
| Parameter / output | Target |
|---|---|
| Treated-zone traffic | −90% or more inside a pedestrianized core with access-only exceptions (residual ~7–10% service/resident traffic) |
| `traffic_evaporation_median` | Citywide motor trips −30% (Concello 1996–2014) to −53% (press) over ~15 years. This is the high end of evaporation, reached with *network-wide* calming plus parking compensation. A single-district FiDi plan should stay well below this (see Ghent/Oslo). |
| Safety | Serious and fatal injuries go to ~0 at 20–30 km/h even with ~800 minor crashes/yr. Severity falls far more than crash count. |
| Residential demand | Centre population +30%, municipality +12% over 20 yrs. Supports a positive residential-amenity effect, but it is not usable as an elasticity. |
| Parking substitution | Remove ~500 central spaces and add >1,000 short-stay free spaces + fringe parking. The sim should model parking *reallocation*, not pure deletion. |
| Café / F&B frontage | ~6x outdoor terraces |
